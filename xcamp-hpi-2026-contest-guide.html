📋 What to Expect at HPI

What it is: The Harker Programming Invitational is an annual team contest run by the Harker Programming Club in San Jose, with both in-person and online participation. This guide uses the HPI 2026 problem set to show what future contests look like.

Duration: About 2 hours of coding — speed on the easy problems matters.

Teams: Up to 3 students. Not ICPC style — every team member codes and submits from their own computer, so teams can split problems and work in parallel.

Divisions: Novice (10 problems, est. CF 800–1900 ≈ USACO Bronze–Gold) and Advanced (10 problems, est. CF 800–2600 ≈ USACO Bronze–Platinum). Many problems are shared — in 2026, 7 of 10 appeared in both divisions.

Rules (2026): Open to all ages. Pre-written templates allowed — prepare yours ahead of time. No generative AI.

Practice now: Both 2026 divisions are on Codeforces Gym — Novice · Advanced. Try a full 2-hour virtual contest as a team to simulate the real thing.

💡 How to Prepare — By Your Current X-Camp Level

Click your level to expand recommendations:

📘 CS101P / CS102P — Python foundations▶
  • Enter the Novice division. Target Harker!! and Skills (Pre-Bronze) — solving both is a great first contest.
  • Stretch: Yash is Cross-Eyed — count each letter and check the counts are even.
📗 CS200 / CS201 / CS202 — Recursion, DFS, BFS▶
  • Enter Novice. Aim for all Pre-Bronze + Bronze problems (Novice A–E).
  • Stretch: Regina’s Task (Novice G) — a direct graph / adjacency-list exercise after CS202.
📙 CS300 / CS301 / CS302 — Silver-level algorithms▶
  • Novice is the sweet spot: target Novice A–G solidly, including the two Silver problems.
  • Stretch: Pace Pushers (Novice I) — sorting + interval merging from CS300.
📕 CS400H / CS401H / CS501 — Gold-level prep▶
  • Move up to Advanced. Aim for Advanced A–G: Tung Tung uses a Fenwick tree, Alysa Liu uses CS501 modular reasoning.
  • Stretch: Daniel Saves Yash (Advanced I) — classic interval DP from CS401H.
📓 CS500H / CS502 — Platinum track▶
  • Enter Advanced and push for A–I. Looksmaxxing (H) is constructive — bound the answer first, then adjust.
  • Tree Queries (J) needs HLD + offline queries — a great long-term goal.
🏆 CS602 / CS603 — US Camp / USACO Platinum▶
  • All 10 Advanced problems are fair game. Tree Queries (J) is your battleground.
  • With 3 people coding in parallel, split the set early: one sweeps A–D, two attack the hard end.
