📋 Contest Format

What it is: A biannual (fall & spring) team programming competition for students up to 12th grade, run by UC Berkeley students.

Structure: 12 problems, most with a main test set plus one or more bonus test sets with larger constraints. Every test set is judged pass/fail — you must pass all cases in a set to earn it.

Limits: Most problems: 1 s / 250 MB. Problem 11: 2 s / 1 GB. Problem 12: 10 s / 250 MB. Language time factors apply (Python gets extra time, but can still TLE on bonus sets).

Strategy: Main test sets are usually much easier than their bonuses — sweep all mains first, then come back for bonuses.

Special: Problem 12 is an open-ended optimization problem (score by quality of answer, not exact correctness) with Easy / Medium / Hard tiers.

Next contest: CALICO Fall ’26 — November 8, 2026. Spring ’26 problems are open for practice on the CALICO judge.

💡 How to Prepare — By Your Current X-Camp Level

Click your level to expand recommendations:

📘 CS101P / CS102P — Python foundations▶
  • Target Problems 1–2 (Pre-Bronze). Read input carefully — both have multiple test cases.
  • Problem 3 (Big Ben Files) is a great stretch: walk through A while tracking your position in B.
📗 CS200 / CS201 / CS202 — Recursion, DFS, BFS▶
  • Aim for Problems 1–3 plus the main sets of 4 and 5 — small constraints mean simulation / brute force works.
  • After CS202, Mycelium main (8) is a direct grid-BFS exercise — good stretch goal.
📙 CS300 / CS301 / CS302 — Silver-level algorithms▶
  • Clear every main set through Problem 8 plus Mycelium Bonus 1. Problems 6 (shadows) and 7 (cross pattern) reward careful reasoning over code.
  • Stretch: Problem 9 (TangTangTang) — DFS through the grid while carrying exact fractions.
📕 CS400H / CS401H / CS501 — Gold-level prep▶
  • Go after the Gold bonuses: Laser bonus (modular inverse from CS501), Grid Fill bonus, Mycelium Bonus 2, and Problem 11 (binary search + 45° rotation).
  • Grab Problem 12 Easy — think in logarithms so a[i]·a[j] becomes log a[i] + log a[j].
📓 CS500H / CS502 — Platinum track▶
  • Push for Platinum sets: Mycelium Bonus 3 (closed-form geometry), Problem 10 (constructive factory), and 11 Bonus.
  • Problem 12 Medium/Hard are open-ended — any improvement over a baseline earns points, so submit early and iterate.
🏆 CS602 / CS603 — US Camp / USACO Platinum▶
  • All 20 test sets are fair game. Problem 12 Hard (2×106 constraints, 10 s) is where top teams separate.
  • Split work across your team: one person sweeps mains, others attack bonuses in parallel.
